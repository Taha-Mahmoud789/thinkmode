## The Core Dynamic

Every field has a load-bearing idea that explains most of what happens around it. In hardware, that idea is that progress is cheaper to announce than to operationalize. The articles in this collection show the gap between the two — and why the gap is where the real work lives. Context for this period is well-documented externally — e.g., [AnandTech](https://www.anandtech.com/) and [IEEE Spectrum Hardware](https://spectrum.ieee.org/topic/computing/hardware/) provide the baseline against which the hardware claim should be judged. Cross-check with a third source before operationalizing.
