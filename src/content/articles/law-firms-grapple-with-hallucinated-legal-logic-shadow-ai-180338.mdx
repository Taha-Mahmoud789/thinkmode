> **Key Takeaways**
> - **Hallucinations have case law:** [Mata v. Avianca, 22-cv-1461 (S.D.N.Y. 2023)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/) — lawyers sanctioned for ChatGPT-fabricated citations — and [60+ sanction motions tracked by Reuters in 2024-2025](https://www.reuters.com/legal/) make hallucinated logic a malpractice vector, not a glitch.
> - **Shadow AI is the bigger risk:** [ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024)](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) warns that associates using unsanctioned tools waive privilege and create undisclosed AI-assisted work product — per [Stanford HAI 2024 on professional AI](https://hai.stanford.edu/research/ai-index).
> - **What disciplined firms build:** A verification firewall — retrieve-then-verify with [Casetext CoCounsel](https://casetext.com/cocounsel/) / [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/products-services/legal.html) + human sign-off per [NIST RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), as we detail in [our ensemble trust piece](/articles/can-multiple-ai-models-improve-enterprise-trust-180481).

What looks like a product update is usually a strategic position stated in hardware or code. AI hallucinations and [shadow AI](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) — unsanctioned tool use by associates — are forcing law firms to rethink legal review, governance, and oversight as court sanctions continue to rise per [Reuters Legal 2024-2025](https://www.reuters.com/legal/).

The position that outlasts the release is verification, not velocity.

## The hallucination that made case law

The cautionary tale is [Mata v. Avianca, Inc., No. 22-cv-1461 (S.D.N.Y. June 22, 2023)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/), where counsel submitted a brief with six fabricated case citations generated by ChatGPT. The court sanctioned the lawyers $5,000 and wrote that the submission reflected "bad faith" — a decision covered by [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/), [ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/), and cited in [ABA Formal Opinion 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) on AI competence.

It wasn't isolated. [Reuters tracked 60+ motions for sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/legal/) involving AI hallucinations 2024-2025, and [Stanford HAI 2024](https://hai.stanford.edu/research/ai-index) found hallucination rates 3-8% even for frontier models on legal QA per [HELM legal](https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/).

![Hallucinated citations — from Mata to 2025 sanctions per Reuters](/article-law-firms-grapple-with-hallucinated-legal-logic-shadow-ai-180338.jpg)

<Callout type="warning" title="The Mata rule">
  If a brief cites a case, a human must have read the case — per [Mata v. Avianca](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/) and [ABA Model Rule 1.1 Competence](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_1_competence/). No AI output goes to court without retrieve-then-verify.
</Callout>

## Shadow AI — the risk you can't see

Hallucinations are visible. Shadow AI is not — an associate pasting privileged matters into a public chatbot, creating a waiver per [ABA Opinion 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) and a data exhaust per [IAPP 2024](https://iapp.org/news/a/shadow-ai-risk/).

| Risk | Hallucinated logic | Shadow AI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Visibility | High — judge catches it per [Mata](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/) | Low — until leak or audit per [ABA 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) |
| Duty implicated | [Rule 1.1 Competence](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_1_competence/) | [Rule 1.6 Confidentiality](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/) |
| Fix | Retrieve-then-verify via [Casetext](https://casetext.com/cocounsel/) / [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/products-services/legal.html) | Allowlisted tools + DLP + logging per [NIST RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) |

Both are the same architectural failure — no verification layer — per [our CTO scaling analysis](/articles/cto-confidence-in-scaling-ai-falls-for-third-straight-year-180411).

## What disciplined firms build

Three controls per [ABA 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) and [NIST RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework):

1. **Allowlisted, logged tools only.** No public chatbot for client matter — use [CoCounsel](https://casetext.com/cocounsel/) or [Microsoft 365 Copilot with Purview](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/solutions/microsoft-purview) with retention per [ABA 512].
2. **Retrieve-then-verify firewall.** Every AI citation must resolve to [Westlaw](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/products/westlaw) / [Lexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/) before human sign-off — the [HELM legal QA cutoff](https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/) as eval.
3. **Attribution in work product.** Disclose AI assistance per [ABA Opinion 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) — the audit trail from our [multi-model trust piece](/articles/can-multiple-ai-models-improve-enterprise-trust-180481).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What was Mata v. Avianca?

A 2023 S.D.N.Y. case where lawyers were sanctioned for submitting ChatGPT-fabricated citations per [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/mata-v-avianca-ai-hallucination-sanctions-2023/) — the precedent cited in [ABA Opinion 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/).

### What is shadow AI in a law firm?

Unsanctioned use of public AI tools for client matters, risking privilege waiver per [ABA Rule 1.6](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/) and [IAPP 2024](https://iapp.org/news/a/shadow-ai-risk/).

### How should firms verify?

Allowlisted tools + retrieve-then-verify against [Westlaw](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/products/westlaw)/[Lexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/) + disclosure per [ABA 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/) and [NIST RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).
